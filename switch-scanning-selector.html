Switch Scanning Selector

Choose a student, load their picture set, then use one switch to scan and select a target.

1. Student and picture library

Folder format: choose a folder for one student containing images named student (correct target) and 1 through 8 (distractors), for example student.jpg, 1.png, 2.jpg. Extra file extensions are fine. You can also upload the files directly for the selected student. Images are stored in this browser on this device, not uploaded to GitHub.
